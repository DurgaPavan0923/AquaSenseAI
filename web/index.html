Platform Capabilities

Calibrated Screening Tiers

Platt-scaled sigmoid probability calibration ensures that predicted risk probabilities accurately correspond to empirical frequencies rather than uncalibrated decision tree scores.

Inductive Conformal Prediction

Constructs distribution-free prediction sets with a 90% finite-sample marginal coverage guarantee. Samples yielding multi-class prediction sets automatically flag: "Needs laboratory test."

Local Occlusion Attribution

Calculates exact probability shifts caused by counterfactual replacement of each parameter with its baseline training median, illuminating chemical drivers behind elevated risks.

How AquaSense AI Works

1. Multi-Parameter Ingestion

Captures eight essential physicochemical and toxicological indicators: pH, Turbidity, Temperature, Dissolved Oxygen, Biochemical Oxygen Demand, Lead, Mercury, and Arsenic.

2. Domain Feature Engineering

Synthesizes statutory hydrological ratios including the Organic Stress Degradation Ratio (BOD/DO), Heavy Metal Hazard Index (HMHI), and Statutory Threshold Breach Count.

3. Disjoint Calibration & Conformal Sets

Predictions pass through Platt probability calibration and conformal non-conformity thresholding calibrated on an independent holdout partition to guarantee exchangeability.

4. Actionable Laboratory Triaging

Clear decision pathways distinguish high-confidence screenings from ambiguous or sensor-fault states, routing urgent contaminated water bodies directly to accredited laboratory assays.

Environmental Monitoring Applications

Municipal Drinking Water Source Protection

Upstream reservoir surveillance to detect early biological or industrial surges before raw water reaches conventional sedimentation and chlorination treatment facilities.

Industrial Effluent & Point-Source Tracking

Rapid verification of heavy metal discharges (Lead, Mercury, Arsenic) and organic loads from manufacturing complexes, ensuring strict statutory compliance with environmental standards.

Agricultural Watershed & Runoff Monitoring

Detection of organic enrichment and hypoxia triggers resulting from chemical fertilizers, livestock waste runoff, and seasonal storm discharge into tributary networks.

River Basin & Wetland Ecology Conservation

Longitudinal monitoring of dissolved oxygen saturation and biochemical oxygen demand to safeguard fish habitats, aquatic macrophyte populations, and wetland biodiversity.
