Academic Research & Methodology

Developed for course URP4201 Research Methodology under the supervision of Dr. Alok Ku. Pani. The project investigates the synthesis of calibrated machine learning and conformal prediction in environmental hydrology.

Dataset & Parameters

1,000 audited water samples with 8 physicochemical and heavy metal features: pH, Turbidity, Temperature, Dissolved Oxygen, BOD, Lead, Mercury, and Arsenic.

Model Methodology

Random Forest ensemble incorporating domain-engineered statutory features (BOD/DO organic stress, HMHI metal hazard index) with Platt sigmoid calibration.

Evaluation Design

4-way disjoint partition (50% train, 15% probability calibration, 15% conformal calibration, 20% holdout test) preventing data leakage and calibration contamination.

Explainability Methodology

Local counterfactual occlusion attribution measuring output probability shifts against training medians, verified for cross-fold rank stability (Spearman rho = 0.9429).

Conformal Uncertainty

Inductive split-conformal prediction sets guaranteeing 90% finite-sample marginal coverage on exchangeable holdout samples without distributional assumptions.

Research Scope & Limits

Screening support tool only. Targets are derived from composite threshold intervals and do not measure microbial or emerging industrial organic contaminants.

Environmental Monitoring Applications

Municipal Drinking Water Source Protection

Upstream reservoir surveillance to detect early biological or industrial surges before raw water reaches conventional sedimentation and chlorination treatment facilities.

Industrial Effluent & Point-Source Tracking

Rapid verification of heavy metal discharges (Lead, Mercury, Arsenic) and organic loads from manufacturing complexes, ensuring strict statutory compliance with environmental standards.

Agricultural Watershed & Runoff Monitoring

Detection of organic enrichment and hypoxia triggers resulting from chemical fertilizers, livestock waste runoff, and seasonal storm discharge into tributary networks.

Educational Laboratories & Fieldwork

Interactive demonstration tool for environmental science and water chemistry students exploring machine learning applications in hydrological risk assessment.
