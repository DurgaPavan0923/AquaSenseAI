Research Overview: URP4201 Research Methodology

AquaSense AI is developed as a final-year AI/ML Senior Design Project investigating the application of calibrated machine learning and conformal prediction sets to environmental water quality monitoring.

Clean freshwater resources face severe threats from organic agricultural runoff, untreated sewage discharges, and industrial heavy metal effluent. Traditional laboratory water assays (mass spectrometry, gas chromatography, 5-day biological oxygen demand incubation) provide high precision but require days of turn-around time. AquaSense AI acts as an initial rapid screening filter to triage water bodies requiring immediate physical intervention or formal regulatory laboratory testing.
