Statutory Water Quality Standards

The decision boundaries and risk evaluation metrics are cross-referenced against authoritative potable water criteria established by the World Health Organization (WHO) and the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS IS 10500:2012).

Methodological Pipeline Architecture

  1. Leakage-Safe Partitioning: Deduplication across feature columns prior to splitting. 80% train and 20% test partition stratified by target risk class with fixed random seeds.
  2. Domain Ratio Engineering: Extraction of physical ratios:
    • Oxygen Saturation Deficit Ratio (OSDR): BOD / (DO + eps)
    • Heavy Metal Hazard Index (HMHI): Normalized statutory sum of Pb, Hg, and As
    • pH Neutrality Deviation: Absolute deviation from neutral pH 7.0
    • Turbidity-DO Ratio: Logarithmic turbidity to dissolved oxygen quotient
    • Toxic Breach Count: Discrete count of simultaneous WHO/BIS limit violations
  3. Cost-Sensitive Ensemble Learning: Random Forest and Tree ensembles trained with balanced class weights to compensate for minority "Moderate" risk instances.
  4. Platt Scaling Calibration: Sigmoidal probability calibration to produce well-calibrated posterior probabilities matching empirical event rates.
  5. Inductive Conformal Prediction: Least Ambiguous Classifier non-conformity scoring guaranteeing 90% finite-sample marginal coverage.
  6. Local Attribution via Occlusion: Fast runtime feature impact evaluation measuring class probability shifts when replacing individual readings with training population medians.
