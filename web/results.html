4-Way Disjoint Partition Breakdown & Leakage Audit

To prevent optimistic bias in probability calibration and inductive conformal quantile derivation, the 1,000-sample dataset was partitioned into four strictly disjoint subsets:

Partition Role Sample Count Proportion Function & Leakage Safeguard
Base Model Training 500 50.0% Fits feature scalers, domain ratios, and base tree ensemble structures.
Probability Calibration 150 15.0% Fits Platt scaling sigmoid calibrator via cross-validated probability outputs.
Conformal Calibration 150 15.0% Derives empirical non-conformity quantile (q_hat = 0.0230) on completely unseen data.
Independent Holdout Test 200 20.0% Unbiased final benchmark for empirical coverage, Macro-F1, and PR-AUC.

Dataset Leakage Audit: Pairwise disjointness verified across all 4 splits (0 shared identifiers, 0 duplicate feature vectors). Preprocessors and scalers fitted exclusively on the base training partition.
