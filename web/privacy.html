Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Student Academic Project Scope

AquaSense AI is an academic research application developed by undergraduate engineering students for course URP4201 Research Methodology. It is not an enterprise commercial entity or certified public testing agency.

2. What Information Is Collected & Processed

When you submit water parameters through the single sample form or upload a CSV file via the batch screening tool, our API processes:

  • Numerical water quality readings (pH, turbidity, temperature, dissolved oxygen, biochemical oxygen demand, lead, mercury, and arsenic).
  • Standard technical request metadata automatically transmitted by your browser (HTTP user-agent and IP address for transient server logging).

3. Data Retention on Hosted Deployments

On hosted serverless deployments (such as Vercel), submitted water parameter values and uploaded CSV files are evaluated entirely in-memory for the duration of the HTTP request. They are not stored, persisted, or logged to permanent server databases.

4. Client-Side Browser Storage

The screening dashboard uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) strictly to maintain your session screening history on your own device. This data never leaves your browser and can be wiped at any time using the "Clear Session History" button.

5. Cookies & Analytics

This website does not use tracking cookies, commercial ad trackers, or third-party profiling scripts.

6. Research Contact

Questions regarding data handling for this academic research project may be directed to the research team:

Email: contact@aquasense-ai.local (or configure CONTACT_EMAIL in environment settings).
